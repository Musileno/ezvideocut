## ⚡ What is EZVideo Cut?

**EZVideo Cut** is a high-speed, automated jump-cut editor tailored for YouTubers, educators, podcasters, and short-form content creators (TikTok / Shorts / Reels). It automatically detects silent pauses in speech and removes them with sample-accurate precision.

### 🌟 Key Features

* **📱 Mobile 720P Online Web Cutter:** Edit vertical (9:16 Shorts/Reels) and landscape clips directly inside your Android / iPhone browser without installing apps.
* **💻 Windows 4K GPU Acceleration:** Harness NVIDIA NVENC, Intel QuickSync QSV, and AMD AMF hardware encoders for 10x-20x faster 4K 60FPS batch rendering.
* **📂 Zero-Lag Batch Queue:** Drag-and-drop dozens of raw footage files from camera SD cards, USB drives, or SSDs.
* **🔒 100% Offline & Private:** All desktop processing runs locally on your PC. No videos are uploaded to the cloud.
* **🆓 Truly 100% Free:** No subscriptions, no trials, and zero watermarks.

## 🚀 Quick Start (Windows Desktop)

1. Download the standalone executable: **[EZVideoCut.exe](https://github.com/Musileno/ezvideocut/releases/download/v1.0.0/EZVideoCut.exe)**
2. Double-click to launch (Portable, zero installation required).
3. Drag & drop your videos into the queue.
4. Click **▶ Toplu Render Başlat** to export jump-cut clean videos directly to your Desktop!
